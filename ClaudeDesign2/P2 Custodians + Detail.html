view_quilt P2 · complete · 8 screens

People-Rows, Entity-Cards,
and one Detail screen.

P2 is in: 7 list screens + 1 detail screen, all running off the same shared CSS. N6 People-Row for people and small entity lists, Entity-Card Grid for highly-visual lists (Modules, Languages, Retention Policies), and N5 Two-Column Detail + Sidecard for single-record overview screens. Sourced from CaseFusion, expressed in _shared/expireon-app.css.

01

Patterns applied across P2

5 building blocks · 8 screens
N6

People-Row Card

Custodians (people) · Containers · Collaboration Links · Internal Domains — with a square .entity-icon variant for non-person entities.

Entity Grid

3- or 4-col Entity Cards

Modules · Retention Categories (3-col) and Languages (4-col with flag-icons + progress bars). For lists where each item has a strong visual identity.

N5

Two-Column Detail + Sidecard

Endpoint Detail — main card with record meta + V4 text-link footer; narrow sidecard with Linked Modules + numeric badge + „Deploy Module“ footer link.

V1

KPI Strip Bar

Used on 6 of 8 screens. One container, internal dividers, no per-cell borders. Cleaner than five separate KPI cards.

V4

Text-link footer actions

Uppercase text-links in primary color (with muted/danger variants) replace solid buttons inside cards everywhere a non-primary action lives.
